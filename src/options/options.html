Help & FAQ

The recognized text has mistakes — is that normal?

Yes, OCR is never 100% perfect.

Columns are merged, or split into too many.

Adjust Column Sensitivity above.

Nothing happens when I click the toolbar icon.

Extractify can't run on chrome:// pages.

"Copy" doesn't seem to do anything.

Some pages restrict clipboard access.

How do I update to a newer version?

Extract the new ZIP over the existing folder.

Does anything leave my computer?

No, everything runs offline.

Why does it need access to every website, and an "offscreen" permission?

Some sites block the OCR engine from starting directly on the page.

Why did it copy something without me clicking Copy?

You turned on auto-copy under Behavior above.
